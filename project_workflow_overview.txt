Park Monitoring System - Project Workflow Overview

The Park Monitoring System is a comprehensive platform designed to manage, maintain, and monitor public parks across various jurisdictions. It bridges the gap between government administration, assigned contractors, and citizens.

Below is the overarching workflow and data structure of the system:

1. Hierarchical Master Data Structure
The system is built on a strict geographical hierarchy to ensure proper delegation and management:
- District (e.g., Bangalore Urban)
- Corporation (e.g., BBMP)
- Zone (e.g., South Zone)
- Ward (e.g., Jayanagar)
- Park (e.g., Madhavan Park)

2. User Roles & Capabilities
The system supports three primary user roles:
- Administrator (Admin): Manages the master data (adding new parks, zones, etc.), onboards new contractors, assigns jurisdictions to contractors, and oversees the entire system through a centralized dashboard.
- Contractor: Third-party personnel or agencies assigned to specific parks or wards. They log into their portal to view assigned parks, check pending maintenance tasks/complaints, and update task statuses. They are profiled based on their "Maintenance Skills" (e.g., Plumbing, Gardening, Electrical).
- Citizen (User): The general public who can view park details, amenities, and report issues (complaints) such as broken benches, non-functional lights, or unkempt gardens.

3. The Core Workflow (Complaints & Maintenance)
- Step A - Issue Identification: A citizen visits a park, notices an issue (e.g., a broken swing), logs into the system, and raises a 'Complaint' against that specific park.
- Step B - Routing & Assignment: The system uses the park's geographical location (Ward/Zone) and the nature of the issue to automatically flag or route the complaint to the designated Contractor assigned to that park.
- Step C - Resolution: The Contractor receives a notification about the pending complaint in their dashboard. They deploy their team to fix the issue.
- Step D - Status Update: Once fixed, the Contractor updates the complaint status to "Resolved" and may upload proof (e.g., photos). 
- Step E - Notification: The Admin and the Citizen who raised the complaint are notified of the resolution via the Announcements/Notifications module.

4. Announcements & Transparency
- The Admin can broadcast Announcements to contractors (e.g., policy changes, general directives) or citizens (e.g., park closure notices).
- This creates a transparent, accountable loop where public infrastructure is actively monitored and maintained efficiently.

5. Recent Updates & UI/UX Workflow Enhancements
A series of significant updates have been deployed to improve both the administrative data capabilities and the citizen-facing user experience:

- Enhanced Park Registration (Admin):
  The administrative workflow for registering new parks has been expanded. Admins can now capture detailed "Visitor Safety & Accessibility" metrics (e.g., Wheelchair Accessible, CCTV Monitoring, First-Aid Facility, Pet Friendly). This data is persisted in the backend schema and rendered directly to the public.

- Modernized Citizen Dashboard Workflow (Public):
  The citizen portal has undergone a major layout refactor to act as a seamless, interactive dashboard:
  * Global Theme: A unified global background image is now persistently visible across all sub-pages (Submit Complaint, Complaint History, Feedback, etc.) using modern glassmorphism (semi-transparent) UI cards, removing solid white blockers.
  * One-Page Park Details: The Park Details page has been structurally compacted. All comprehensive park information (including the new Safety & Accessibility metrics and uniform Material Icons for facilities) and primary action buttons ("Report an Issue", "Give Feedback") now fit entirely on a single screen without the need for vertical scrolling.
  * Smart Events Display: The Events page workflow has been refined to dynamically hide empty sections (e.g., hiding the "Upcoming Events" block entirely if none exist) to prevent user confusion.
  
These updates streamline the citizen's journey from viewing park amenities to seamlessly tracking complaints and reporting issues within a clean, single-page dashboard experience.
